# Taal Trek - Dutch Language Learning App

## Support & Help

Welcome to Taal Trek! This app helps you learn Dutch vocabulary and grammar through interactive flashcards, exercises, and study modes.

### 📱 App Features

- **Flashcard Learning**: Create and study custom Dutch vocabulary cards
- **Interactive Exercises**: Multiple choice, fill-in-the-blank, and sentence building exercises
- **Store Content**: Access curated vocabulary packs and exercises by chapter
- **Study Modes**: Advanced study sessions with progress tracking
- **Audio Support**: Listen to Dutch pronunciation
- **Progress Tracking**: Monitor your learning progress with XP and statistics
- **Import/Export**: Share your flashcards and exercises with others

### 🆘 Common Issues & Solutions

#### **App Crashes or Freezes**
- Close the app completely and restart it
- Ensure you have the latest version from the App Store
- Restart your device if the issue persists

#### **Can't Import Cards or Exercises**
- Check that your CSV file is properly formatted
- Ensure the file has the correct headers (Word, Definition, Example, etc.)
- Try importing a smaller batch of cards first

#### **Audio Not Playing**
- Check your device's volume settings
- Ensure you have granted microphone permissions
- Try restarting the app

#### **Progress Not Saving**
- Make sure you're connected to the internet
- Check that you have sufficient storage space
- Try closing and reopening the app

#### **Store Content Not Loading**
- Check your internet connection
- Try refreshing the store page
- Restart the app if the issue persists

### 📋 CSV Import Format

For importing vocabulary cards, use this format:
```csv
Word,Definition,Example,Article
hond,dog,De hond is groot.,de
kat,cat,De kat slaapt.,de
```

For importing exercises, use this format:
```csv
Word,Exercise Type,Question,Correct Answer,Options,Explanation
hond,Multiple Choice,What does hond mean?,Dog,Dog;Cat;Bird;Fish,Hond means dog in Dutch.
```

### 🎯 Study Tips

1. **Start Small**: Begin with basic vocabulary before moving to complex grammar
2. **Regular Practice**: Study for 10-15 minutes daily rather than long sessions
3. **Use Audio**: Listen to pronunciations to improve your speaking
4. **Create Personal Cards**: Add words you encounter in daily life
5. **Review Regularly**: Use the spaced repetition feature to retain information
