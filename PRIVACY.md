# Privacy Policy for Taal Trek

**Last updated**: January 2025

## Introduction

Taal Trek ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our Dutch language learning application.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
Taal Trek is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information such as:
- Names
- Email addresses
- Phone numbers
- Physical addresses
- Social media accounts
- Payment information

### App Usage Data
The app may collect anonymous usage data to improve functionality:
- Learning progress and statistics
- Feature usage patterns
- App performance metrics
- Crash reports (if enabled)

### User-Generated Content
All content you create within the app is stored locally on your device:
- Flashcards you create
- Study progress and statistics
- Custom vocabulary lists
- Exercise results

## How We Use Information

### Local Storage
All your learning data is stored locally on your device:
- Your flashcards and vocabulary lists
- Study progress and statistics
- Custom exercises and content
- App preferences and settings

### Data Processing
We do not process, analyze, or share your personal data. Any anonymous usage data collected is used solely to:
- Improve app performance
- Fix bugs and issues
- Enhance user experience
- Develop new features

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

### No Third-Party Sharing
We do not share, sell, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your data remains on your device and is not transmitted to external servers.

### Legal Requirements
We may disclose information only if required by law or to protect our rights and safety.

## Data Security

### Local Storage Security
- All data is stored locally on your device
- No data is transmitted to external servers
- Your information is protected by your device's security measures

### App Security
- We implement industry-standard security practices
- Regular security updates and patches
- Secure coding practices

## Your Rights and Choices

### Data Control
You have complete control over your data:
- All data is stored locally on your device
- You can delete the app to remove all data
- You can export your flashcards and progress
- You can import data from other sources

### Opt-Out Options
- Disable crash reporting in app settings
- Turn off analytics (if applicable)
- Choose not to use cloud sync features

## Children's Privacy

Taal Trek is suitable for users of all ages, but we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

## International Users

Taal Trek is available worldwide. By using our app, you consent to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by:
- Updating the "Last updated" date
- Posting the new Privacy Policy in the app
- Notifying users through app updates

## Data Retention

### Local Data
- Your data remains on your device until you delete the app
- No data is retained on our servers
- You can export your data at any time

### Anonymous Usage Data
- Anonymous usage data is retained for app improvement purposes
- This data cannot be linked to individual users
- Data is automatically deleted after a reasonable period

## App Permissions

Taal Trek may request the following permissions:

### Camera Permission
- **Purpose**: To scan and import text from images
- **Usage**: Only when you actively use the photo import feature
- **Data**: Images are processed locally and not stored

### Microphone Permission
- **Purpose**: To record pronunciation for flashcards
- **Usage**: Only when you actively record audio
- **Data**: Audio is stored locally on your device only

### Storage Permission
- **Purpose**: To import/export flashcards and data
- **Usage**: Only when you actively import or export files
- **Data**: Files are accessed locally and not transmitted

## Third-Party Services

### Google ML Kit
- **Purpose**: Text recognition from images
- **Data**: Images are processed locally
- **Privacy**: No data is sent to Google servers

### Audio Players
- **Purpose**: Play pronunciation audio
- **Data**: Audio files are stored locally
- **Privacy**: No data is transmitted

## Data Export and Portability

You can export your data at any time:
- Export flashcards as CSV files
- Export progress and statistics
- Backup your learning data
- Transfer data between devices

## Data Deletion

To delete all your data:
1. Delete the app from your device
2. All local data will be removed
3. No data remains on our servers
4. No recovery is possible after deletion

## Compliance

This Privacy Policy complies with:
- App Store guidelines
- GDPR requirements (for EU users)
- CCPA requirements (for California users)
- General privacy best practices
